Gelo Recommendations

Semantic, context-aware recommendations for Jellyfin: home shelves and sub-ms item-to-item suggestions tuned from your watch history. Defaults are sensible for most libraries — change only what you want to diverge. Relevance weights should sum to ~1.0.
